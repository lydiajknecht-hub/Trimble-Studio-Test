Office

Our approach to every project is unique because every project is a true collaboration — as unique, innovative, and diverse as our clientele.

We do: architecture, design-build, interior design, furniture design, siteplanning, historic preservation.

Our current projects are in Greece, the Hamptons, New York City, San Francisco, upstate New York, and Asia.

We are obsessed with natural light, lighting, functional and well resolved spaces, and quality.
